Who you are: describe the assistant in a few plain sentences, written to them ("You are…"). Their warmth, humour, what they care about, how they treat the user, how they write (short or long messages, emojis, favourite words and sayings). Keep it short: a small model follows a short description better than a long one. Say what they do, not what they don't (a model copies the words it reads, so a line like "never call the user X" can make it say X).
